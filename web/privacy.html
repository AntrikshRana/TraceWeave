PRIVACY

Privacy notice

This page explains what TraceWeave receives when you use the hosted workspace and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

When you create an account, TraceWeave receives your email address and the authentication details handled by Supabase Auth. When you add logs, the service receives the source name, original log content, normalized fields, review decisions, and processing history needed to provide the workspace.

How it is used

Your information is used to sign you in, process and display your logs, preserve traceability to original content, and provide exports. We do not sell your information or use your log content for advertising.

Where it is kept

The hosted workspace uses a Supabase project for account-scoped workspace data. Access is limited by your authenticated session. Authentication cookies are HttpOnly and Secure; short-lived cookies are also used during Google sign-in. The local version of the application keeps its own data on the computer running it.

Your choices

You can export your processed records from the workspace and stop using the service at any time. To request account or workspace deletion, contact the project owner through the TraceWeave repository. Do not include passwords or access tokens in a request.

Changes and questions

We may update this notice when the service changes. The date below identifies the current version.
